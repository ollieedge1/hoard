Questions

Does Hoard need my bank login?

No, and it never will. Hoard has no account and no server. Everything is worked out on your device from two numbers you already know: your salary and your pension percentage.

Is Hoard free?

Yes. The budget, a full retirement check, the tax code check and every guide are free. Hoard Pro (£1.99 a month, £19.99 a year, or £34.99 once) unlocks the living version: a track that updates monthly, the growing hoard, drawdown odds, and unlimited payslip and statement scanning.

What does the pension boost actually do?

It finds the contribution rate that fits your goal, whether that's keeping your Child Benefit, escaping the 60% tax trap, staying out of the higher rate, or simply putting in as much as you can afford. Then it gives you the exact words for payroll, and the two questions worth asking while you're there.

How does the tax code check work?

Hoard checks your code against your salary and tells you whether it looks right, is worth a look, or looks wrong, with an estimate of what an error is costing you a year. Errors are common, and correcting them with HMRC is free and routine.

Can Hoard find my old pensions?

It points you at the government's free Pension Tracing Service, then reads the statements you scan so your hoard runs on real figures. Before showing you any provider, it lays out the honest case for and against combining, including the guarantees you could lose.

Is this financial advice?

No. Hoard gives you information and guidance so you can make your own decisions, and always signposts the free official route (MoneyHelper, HMRC, gov.uk) where that's the better answer.
