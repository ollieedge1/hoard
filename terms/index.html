Information, not advice

Hoard gives general information, calculations and guidance to help you understand your pay, budget and pension and make your own decisions. It is not regulated financial advice, and nothing in the app, including your pal's chat answers, is a personal recommendation to buy, sell, transfer or change anything.

Hoard is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

For free, impartial help, try MoneyHelper. If you're 50 or over and have a defined contribution pension, you can book a free Pension Wise appointment. For a personal recommendation, use a regulated financial adviser; you can check one on the FCA register.

Estimates and accuracy

Hoard's figures are estimates. They depend on:

  • what you enter, and what the app reads from payslips and statements you scan
  • UK income tax and National Insurance rules for the current tax year, including Scottish income tax rates where they apply
  • assumptions about things nobody can know for certain, such as investment growth, inflation, your future pay and the State Pension

Real outcomes will differ, and tax rules change. Scans can misread a figure, and chat answers are generated automatically and can be wrong. Please check anything important against your payslip, pension statement, your employer's payroll team or HMRC before you act on it, especially before changing your pension contributions, salary sacrifice or tax code, or taking money from a pension.

Hoard Pro

Much of Hoard is free. Hoard Pro unlocks extra features, which are described in the app before you buy. You can choose:

  • Monthly or Yearly: an auto-renewing subscription
  • Lifetime: a single payment that unlocks Hoard Pro for as long as Hoard is available on the App Store, with nothing more to pay

The price, in your currency, is shown in the app and by Apple before you confirm. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, and you can restore them on another iPhone with Restore Purchases on the Hoard Pro screen.

Subscriptions and cancelling

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
  • Your Apple Account is charged for the next period within the 24 hours before the current one ends.
  • To cancel or switch plans, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.
  • When you cancel, you keep Hoard Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • If a subscription price goes up, Apple will tell you first and, where required, ask you to agree before you're charged the new price.
